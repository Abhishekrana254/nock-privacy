Privacy policy for Nock

ARana Studio. Last updated 6 October 2026.

Nock is a puzzle game. This page explains what the app does with information.

Information the app handles

Nock does not ask you to create an account. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

Your level progress is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes that progress. It is not sent to ARana Studio.

Ads

The current game does not show ads and does not include an advertising kit. If ads are added later, this page will be updated before that version is published. An ad provider such as Google AdMob may then receive a device advertising identifier, as described in that provider’s own policy.

Sharing

ARana Studio does not sell personal information. The app does not send your progress to other companies.

Children

Nock is a general puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
